## 2. Plain Text / Letter-Style Note
**Purpose:** Personal storytelling, new product context, exclusive announcements

**Structure:**
- Eyebrow (optional): "From Bobbi" or "Hi there"
- Hero: Hed / short intro paragraph. Always includes First Name
- Body: 1–3 paragraphs (focus on context or education)
- CTAs: There are from 4 to 5 CTAs within the text, centered and minimal and there's always 1 CTA at the end with some "urgency" "scarcity" tone.

**Example Structure:**
- SL: A Note From Bobbi: Why I Love Minis / PH: Get Her Exclusive Faves
- Hero: Hi there — Bobbi here.
- Body: Personal story/context (1-3 paragraphs with embedded CTAs)
- Sign-off: xX Bobbi

## 3. Product Spotlight / Hero Product
**Purpose:** Feature one product and its benefits

**Structure:**
- Hero: Product name or strong benefit statement / short Dek / CTA
- Section 1: What It Is (concise)
- Section 2: Why We Love It (feature bullets or icons), Who it's for (if applicable) or When to use it
- Section 3 (optional): Shades and quiz
- Section 3 (optional): Review callout or application tip
- CTA: Focusing on the name of the product or its benefit
- Footer: Related product or upsell (ideally application tools)

**Example Structure:**
- SL: Dry January? Not With WTF / PH: Ultimate Moisture for Dry Winter Skin
- Hero: Hed + Dek + CTA
- Section 1: The 411 on Using WTF (usage tips with CTAs)
- Footer: Not Sure About Your Shade? (quiz CTA)

## 4. Product Roundup / Theme-Based Edit
**Purpose:** Tie multiple products around a need (summer skin, color pops, x ways to do x, etc.)

**Structure:**
- Hero: Theme title (ex. "Travel-Friendly Musts") / Dek / CTA
- Module per product:
  - Name
  - One-sentence benefit
  - CTA (ex. "Shop Product Name" or "Shop the X Shades")
- CTA: A closing CTA for all the section
- Optional: Pro tip or quiz to find your shades
- Footer: Link to full collection or related collections

**Example Structure:**
- SL: Pack a Beach Bag With Us / PH: Save Some Room for SPF
- Hero: Heading to the Beach? Take Us With You
- Section 1: Beach Bag Must-Haves (product list with benefits)
- Footer: PTO? Scheduled. SPF? On.

## 9. Promotional Email (e.g. GWP, Sale)
**Purpose:** Drive conversion via urgency

**Structure:**
- Eyebrow (mandatory): "Limited Time" or "Ends Soon"
- Hero: Hed focuses on the benefit (not just "Sale")
- Subhed: Precise offer details (ex. "Free Lip Tint on Orders $100+")
- Dek: Seasonal or soft-selling phrase plus promo description
- Section 1: How it works and/or featured eligible products
- CTA: "Shop the Sale"
- Footer: Reminder of "Limited time" or exclusivity
- Disclaimer: Promo terms and conditions

**Example Structure:**
- SL: Get The Lip Tint, On Us / PH: Don't Miss It: Order Today for Mother's Day Delivery
- Hero: A Little Something to Celebrate the Moms + Sub + Dek + CTA
- Section 1: Simply Choose Your Fave Shade (options)
- Footer: Get Your Gifts On Time (shipping deadlines)
- Disclaimer: Terms and conditions

## 11. How-To (Problem/Solution)
**Purpose:** Educate on how to fix a common skin/makeup problem

**Structure:**
- Hero: "How to XX"
- Dek: Call out the problem + solutions ahead
- Section 1: Problem → Product(s) that solve it, with brief copy
- Section 2: Optional step-by-step or pro tip
- Footer: Cross-sell related products or shop all

**Example Structure:**
- SL: Avoiding Eyeliner? Try Gel Liner / PH: A Fave For Hooded Eyes, Oily Lids, & Makeup Newbies
- Hero: No Smudging or Budging + Dek + CTA
- Module 1: A Solution For Hooded & Deep-Set Eyes (with Bobbi quote)

- Footer: The Mascara (complementary product)

## BEST PRACTICES FOR ALL FRAMEWORKS

### General Guidelines:
- **Intro Dek:** Max 2 sentences or 108 characters
- **CTAs:** Action-first, benefit-forward ("Try It Now," "See All Shades," "Get the Set")
- **Headlines:** Clear over clever (great if you can do both)
- **Tone:** Conversational, not salesy
- **Audience:** Write to one person, not "our customers"
- **Focus:** Product-focused, not brand-first (benefit-led)

### Seasonal Approach:
- Use environment-setting intros ("Long days. Warm nights. Light layers.")
- Use seasonal language to make evergreen products feel timely (e.g., "Brighten up for summer")
- Include badges in the products that are new or exclusive

### Content Specifications:
- **Reviews/Quotes:** From 2 to 4 lines, 250 characters max
- **Subject Lines:** Always include 2 versions of Subject Line and Preheader
- **Emojis:** Don't use emojis
- **CTAs:** Strategic placement throughout copy, not just at the end
