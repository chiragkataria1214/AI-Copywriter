Persona Breakdown

Innovators
Profile: Early adopters who are always on the lookout for revolutionary products. They love being the first to try something new and sharing their discoveries.
Motivations: They’re driven by curiosity and the desire to stay ahead of beauty trends. They see Jones Road as a trailblazer in clean beauty.
Skin Solutionist
Profile: Individuals with specific skin concerns (e.g., sensitivity, acne, dryness) looking for gentle, effective solutions.
Motivations: They want products that address their skin issues without compromising on quality or clean ingredients.
Beauty Novice
Profile: Those new to makeup or returning after a long hiatus, looking for approachable, foolproof products.
Motivations: They’re seeking products that are easy to use and help them feel confident.
Trend Seeker
Profile: Social media-savvy individuals drawn to viral products and beauty trends.
Motivations: They want to stay current and feel connected to the beauty community.
Luxury for Less
Profile: Shoppers who desire high-quality, premium beauty products but at a reasonable price.
Motivations: They value indulgence but appreciate the practicality of affordable luxury.
Minimalist
Profile: Individuals who prefer a streamlined routine with multi-functional products.
Motivations: They’re drawn to simplicity, efficiency, and sustainability.
Skincare Enthusiast
Profile: Passionate about skin health and prioritizing clean, nourishing products.
Motivations: They seek products that enhance their skincare routine and prioritize ingredients.
Life Juggler
Profile: Busy individuals balancing work, family, and personal life, looking for reliable, time-saving beauty solutions.
Motivations: They want products that fit seamlessly into their packed schedules and provide effortless results.
Sub-Personas:
New Mom (6 month postpartum) - first time struggling + guilt; ingredient focused
Key emotions: guilt, no time for self-care, hormone changes
Repeat Mom - trying to maintain routine, worried about disruption
Key emotions: stress, fear of losing established routine
Professional Mom - innovative, checking things off checklist; type A
Key emotions: busy, guilt about self-care, needs quick solutions
Wellness/Stay-at-home Mom (aspirational persona)
Key focus: clean ingredients for whole family, ingredient research
Beauty Enthusiast
Profile: Passionate about all things beauty, from trying the latest trends to experimenting with new looks.
Motivations: They view beauty as a form of self-expression and enjoy exploring innovative products.
