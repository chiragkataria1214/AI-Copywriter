## **15 Email Types & Their Frameworks**

### **1. GTL (Get the Look)**
- **Purpose**: Show complete look recreation using multiple products
- **Structure**: Hero → Intro Module → Look Breakdown → Footer
- **Key Elements**: Step-by-step product application, seasonal inspiration, pro tips

### **2. Plain Text / Letter-Style Note**
- **Purpose**: Personal storytelling, product context, exclusive announcements
- **Structure**: Personal greeting → Context paragraphs → Multiple CTAs → Urgency close
- **Tone**: Conversational, first-person from founder

### **3. Product Spotlight / Hero Product**
- **Purpose**: Feature single product and benefits
- **Structure**: Hero benefit → What It Is → Why We Love It → Shades/Application → Footer
- **Focus**: Problem-solution positioning

### **4. Product Roundup / Theme-Based Edit**
- **Purpose**: Multiple products around a theme/need
- **Structure**: Theme title → Product modules → Closing CTA → Footer
- **Examples**: Travel essentials, makeup bag must-haves

### **5. Back in Stock**
- **Purpose**: Announce product returns
- **Structure**: "It's Back" eyebrow → Product name → Brief description → CTA
- **Tone**: Excitement and urgency

### **6. Product Launch**
- **Purpose**: New product introduction
- **Structure**: NEW positioning → What It Is → Benefits → Shades → Footer
- **Key**: Always lead with "NEW" or "INTRODUCING"

### **7. Teaser Email (Pre-Launch)**
- **Purpose**: Build anticipation
- **Structure**: Vague enticing headline → Small hint → Preview CTA → SMS signup
- **Strategy**: Create curiosity without revealing everything

### **8. Retail Event / Pop-Up / IRL Activation**
- **Purpose**: Drive in-person attendance
- **Structure**: Event name → Details (What/Where/When) → RSVP CTA → Online exclusives
- **Focus**: Exclusivity and personal experience

### **9. Promotional Email**
- **Purpose**: Drive conversion via urgency
- **Structure**: "Limited Time" eyebrow → Benefit-focused headline → Offer details → How it works → Terms
- **Key**: Never lead with just "Sale" - focus on customer benefit

### **10. Set or Kit Email**
- **Purpose**: Promote curated bundles
- **Structure**: Set name → What's included → Why you'll love it → Limited time reminder
- **Value Prop**: Convenience and value bundling

### **11. How-To (Problem/Solution)**
- **Purpose**: Educate on solving common problems
- **Structure**: Problem identification → Product solutions → Step-by-step → Cross-sell
- **Approach**: Educational first, selling second

### **12. Duos or Product Combinations**
- **Purpose**: Sell complementary products
- **Structure**: Combo benefit → Product 1 spotlight → Product 2 spotlight → Pro tip → Related products
- **Strategy**: Show enhanced results from pairing

### **13. Shade Roundup**
- **Purpose**: Curate shades for seasons/moods
- **Structure**: Season/mood theme → Shade spotlights → Benefits → Shop all
- **Focus**: Emotional connection to colors

### **14. How to Use It (Product Tutorial)**
- **Purpose**: Teach application/layering
- **Structure**: Product uniqueness → Step-by-step instructions → Pro tips → Tools/accessories
- **Goal**: Education leading to confidence and purchase

### **15. Social Proof**
- **Purpose**: Build credibility through reviews/press
- **Structure**: "What people are saying" → 3-4 testimonials → Product CTA
- **Types**: Customer reviews or press quotes

## **Universal Framework Elements**

### **Email Structure Components:**
- **Eyebrow**: Optional context setter
- **Hero**: Main headline + subhead + CTA
- **Body Sections**: 1-3 focused modules
- **Footer**: Related products or urgency reminder
- **Disclaimer**: When needed for promotions

### **CTA Best Practices:**
- Action-first, benefit-forward
- Examples: "Try It Now," "See All Shades," "Get the Set"
- Multiple CTAs throughout (4-5 in letter-style emails)

## **Writing Guidelines**

### **Tone & Voice:**
- Conversational, not salesy
- Write to one person, not "customers"
- Product-focused, not brand-first
- Benefit-led positioning

### **Technical Specifications:**
- Intro Dek: Max 2 sentences or 108 characters
- Reviews/Quotes: 2-4 lines, 250 characters max
- No emojis
- Always include 2 versions of subject line and preheader

### **Subject Line Best Practices:**
- Clear product mentions perform best
- Urgency and time-sensitivity work
- Short, direct preview texts (4-6 words)
- Avoid general or inspirational copy
- Benefit-first framing
- Be specific about products and shades

## **Segmentation Strategy**
- **Prospects**: Introduction and education-focused
- **Existing Customers**: Exclusivity and differentiation-focused
- **Product-Specific**: Customized based on previous purchases

## **Seasonal Considerations**
- Use environment-setting intros
- Make evergreen products feel timely
- Include seasonal language and relevant occasions
- Highlight limited-edition or exclusive items
